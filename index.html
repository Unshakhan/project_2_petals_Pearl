At Petals & Pearl, every piece is thoughtfully handmade using beautiful crystals, beads and carefully selected details. Our creations are designed to add a delicate touch of elegance to everyday moments, meaningful gifts and special occasions.

From our small studio in Karachi, each order receives personal attention — whether it's a piece from our collection or a custom design shaped around your idea.
